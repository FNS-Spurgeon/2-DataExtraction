1896. Manly, John Matthews. On the Date and Interpretation of
Chaucer's Complaint of Mars, [in] Harvard Studies and Notes in
Philology and Literature, Boston, Mass., 1896 [issued 1897], vol. v,
pp. 107-26. [Attacks Turcin's conclusions, Anglia, ix, 582-4,
A.D. 1379,—with astrological data and calculation.]
[p.124] I think I have shown that the poem is so packed with
astrological allusions and conforms so closely to astronomical
relations and movements, that it can hardly be regarded as
anything else than a mere exercise of ingenuity in describing
a supposed astronomical event in terms of human action and
emotion.
