1900. Skeat, Walter W. The Chaucer Canon, with a Discussion of the
Works associated with the name of Geoffrey Chaucer. 1900.
"The object of this treatise is to explain clearly the chief
peculiarities of Chaucer's grammar and versification . . . In
this way the true Canon of Chaucer's Works can be compiled
with ease and certainty, and can no longer be controverted, in
the future, except by such as deny the existence of arguments
which it is inconvenient to understand."
