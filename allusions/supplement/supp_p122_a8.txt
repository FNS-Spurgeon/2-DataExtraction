1891. Skeat, Walter W. A print in pamphlet form, of To Rosemunde.
[This poem, by Chaucer, was discovered in the Bodleian on
April 2, 1891, by Prof. Skeat, on the last leaf of a fifteenth¬
century copy of Chaucer’s Troilus, written out by a scribe
named 'Tregentil,' according to Prof. Skeat’s interpretation
of that word. That 'Tregentil' was a name is not now
accepted. See Dr. McCracken’s article in Athenæum, Feb¬
ruary 29, 1908, p. 258.]
