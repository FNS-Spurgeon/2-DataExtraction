1894. Morris, William. Letter to the Editor of The Daily Chronicle,
dated Kelmscott Press, Hammersmith, July 20 [printed in] The
Daily Chronicle, July 24, 1894, p. 5, col. 4.
[A short note, referring to the notice of July 20 (see below),
and saying that it would be an encouragement to those who
have already subscribed for the "Chaucer" to know that the
work is advancing rapidly, and that he hopes to issue it about
the middle of the following year (1895).]
A similar notice appears in The Academy, July 28, 1894,
vol. xliv, p. 65, col. 3.
