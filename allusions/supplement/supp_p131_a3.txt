1894. Morris, William. Letter to his daughter Jenny, [11 Aug. 1894,
printed in] The Collected Works of William Morris, with intro¬
duction by his daughter May Morris, London, 1912, vol. xv, p. xxx.
Nothing has happened since yesterday except that we
printed the first side of Chaucer on vellum; successfully, I
think, after some trouble. I have done a big O for the Man
of Laws Tale.
