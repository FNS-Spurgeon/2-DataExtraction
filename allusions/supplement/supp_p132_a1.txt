1894. Mott, Lewis Freeman. The Naïveté of Chaucer [in] The Academy,
London, vol. xlv, May 5, 1894, p. 373, cols. 1, 2.
[An interesting note, maintaining, with illustrative examples,
that Chaucer's naïveté is not to be ascribed, as is often done,
to his archaic language, but to his genius; and that it is a
poetic quality of a high order.]
