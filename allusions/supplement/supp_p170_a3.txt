1900. Unknown. A Bust of Chaucer for Guildhall. Daily News,
Oct. 19, 1900, p. 9, col. 1.
Yesterday a meeting of the Court of Common Council was
held at Guildhall, the Lord Mayor presiding.
At the instance of the Library Committee, it was agreed to
erect a bust, in marble or bronze, of Chaucer, the father of
English poetry, in the library or art gallery at Guildhall at an
expense not exceeding £10.
