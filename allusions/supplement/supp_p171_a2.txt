1900. Unknown. The Poet Laureate on Chaucer, [a column in] The
Times, Oct. 26, 1900, p. 6, col. iii.
[An account of the unveiling of the memorial window, by
Mr. C. E. Kempe, in the Collegiate Church of St. Saviour,
Southwark, on Oct. 25, 1900, the 500th anniversary of the
death of Chaucer. Mr. Alfred Austin delivered an address
on Chaucer. He touched on the changes his reputation has
passed through, and said his position was now established.
He represented the English type of character and intellect, in
his love of nature, his humour, &c. "Poets like Chaucer
were themselves ministers of God."]
