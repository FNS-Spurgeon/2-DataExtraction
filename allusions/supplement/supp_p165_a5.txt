1900. Ker, William Paton. Review of The Chaucer Canon, by
W. W. Skeat, [in] Bookman, London, June 1900, vol. xviii,
pp. 93-4.
[p. 94] Professor Skeat occasionally allows some emotions to intrude
among his scientific demonstrations, and perhaps at times
insists too much on the cogency of his arguments ; one gets
the impression that he is arguing fervently with an unbelieving
audience who refuse to consider their final e's.
