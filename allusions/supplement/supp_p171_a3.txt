1900. Hodson. WOLVERhampton. A Chaucer Collection, [notice of
above under the heading] Library Notes and News, [in] The Library
Association Record, December, 1900, vol. ii, part II, p. 662.
An account of "a unique collection of Chaucer manuscripts
being exhibited at the Wolverhampton Free Library by
Councillor Laurence Hodson," who for many years past has
collected the works of the father of English poetry." There
are 14 cases in all, and the exhibit includes "a copy of the
Canterbury Tales—the Norton MSS.—written prior to 1425;
the Ashburnham MSS. and a number of autotypes of MSS.
published by the Chaucer Society.]
