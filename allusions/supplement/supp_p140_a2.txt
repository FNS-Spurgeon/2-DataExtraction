1895. Hempl, George. Review of A. W. Pollard’s Chaucer's Canterbury
Tales (2 vols.), [in] Modern Language Notes, March 1895,
pp. 89-90.
[General disapproval of the work, with the comment that
the notes are too scanty and some examples. Pollard's idea
of purity a Chaucer in modern spelling is condemned. The
conclusion is :]
The edition is a good one for those whom the editor had
in mind: persons of literary tastes who want to know some-
thing about Chaucer without bothering to find out just what
he said and how he said it.
