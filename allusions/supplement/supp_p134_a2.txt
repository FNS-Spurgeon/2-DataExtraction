1894. Skeat, W. W., and Pollard, A. W. An Unknown Balade by
Chaucer, [in] Athenæum, June 9, etc., 1894, pp. 742, 773, 805, 837.
[The balade is 'Balade that Chaucer made,' from Addit.
MS. 34360, fol. 21b, beginning:
—3 stanzas of nine, aabaabab and Envoy,—
'So hath my hert[e] caught in remembraunce,'
Skeat claiming to have discovered it and acclaiming it as
Chaucer’s, Pollard describing it as previously known and
unchaucerian.]
[The 'balade,' with Skeat's note, was reprinted in The
Critic, New York, July 14, 1894.]
