1898. Petersen, Kate O. On the Sources of the Nonne Prestes Tale,
Radcliffe College Monographs. No. 10.
[A detailed essay, 144 pp., the contents are:
I. The Cock and Fox Story in Learned, in Oral, and in
Epic tradition.
II. The Theme of the Pursuit by Dogs.
III. The Captive’s Trick.
IV. The Captor's Trick.
V. The Version from which Chaucer derived his material.

Nonne Prestes Tale.]
