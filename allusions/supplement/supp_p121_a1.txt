1891. Morley, Henry. Playful Poems, edited by Henry Morley
(Routledge’s Companion Poets, No. VII), London, 1891, pp. vii-xii,
17-73.
[The Introduction deals first of all with the 1841 edition of
the modernizations of Chaucer by Leigh Hunt, Wordsworth,
etc., and Sir Thopas; and the Chaucerian pieces reprinted are
the Manciple’s Tale, modernized by Leigh Hunt; Rime of Sir
Thopas, modernized by Z. A. Z.; Friar’s Tale, modernized by
Leigh Hunt; Reve’s Tale, modernised by R. H. Horne; and
Cuckoo and Nightingale, modernized by Wordsworth.]
