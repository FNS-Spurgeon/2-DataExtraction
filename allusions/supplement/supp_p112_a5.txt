1889. McClumpha, Charles Flint. Chaucer's Dream [in] Modern
Language Notes, Baltimore, 1889, vol. iv, pp. 65-67.
[The article notes Brandl’s  "Uber einige historische An¬
spielungen in den Chaucer-Dichtungen" (Engl. Studien, Heft 2,
Bd. xii); Herzberg (Lemcke’s Jahrb viii, 133); ten Brink
(Ch. Studien, i, 165) ; Skeat (Prioresses T., p. lxxxi.) ; and
gives data on the imperfections in ryme. From the evidence
McChumpha would conclude that Chaucer was not the
author, and that the original dialect of the Dream was not
Northumbrian, but a Midland dialect exhibiting Northumbrian
tendencies.]
