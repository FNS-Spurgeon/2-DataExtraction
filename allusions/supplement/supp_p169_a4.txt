[A light and charming paper written 'to the unlearned'
with 'the object of winning some new readers for this great
and lovable master'—Chaucer's versification misunderstood by
Spenser and Dryden—the points in which the old critics were
content to praise Chaucer—Dryden's criticism and modernisa-
tions—single lines of beauty and telling phrases in Chaucer
—Chaucer's skill in expression in The Knight's Tale—His
view of human life—Specimens of his humour.]
