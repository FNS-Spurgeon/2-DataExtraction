1894. Kittredge, George Lyman. Observations on the Language of
Chaucer's Troilus, Chaucer Society, London, 1894 [issue for 1891.]
(Also issued as vol. iii of Harvard Studies and Notes in Philology
and Literature, Boston, 1894.)
[A detailed (426 pp.) and most valuable linguistic study,
based on the readings of four MSS.: Campsall, Harl. 2280;
Camb., Gg. 4, 27; and Harl. 3943, all as edited for the
Chaucer Society by Dr. Furnivall. Under the Grammatical
Chapter the forms of Nouns, Adjectives, Pronouns, Adverbs,
and other Particles, and of Verbs, are discussed. Then
follows the "Metrical Chapter." A Comparative Table of
Line-Numbers is given, showing the correspondence between
Furnivall’s Campsall Text and Morris's Aldine edition.]
