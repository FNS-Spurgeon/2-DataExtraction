1896. Unknown. Review of the Kelmscott Chaucer, [in] The Nation
New York, vol. lxiii, Sept. 3, 1896, pp. 173-4.
[The volume is, on the whole, rather a disappointment. It
is heavy and difficult to read.]
We... wonder what Chaucer would have thought of so
sad a company.  ... Imagine, if you can, the wife of Bath
according to Burne-Jones-fortunately he has made no
endeavour to depict her–and you will realize how little fitted
he is by temperament for the work.
