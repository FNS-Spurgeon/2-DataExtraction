1891. Schelling, Felix E. Obituary. John G. R. McElroy [in]
Modern Language Notes, Baltimore, 1891, vol. vi, p. 31.
Only last summer he [McElroy] was invited by Dr.
Furnivall to undertake the "Variorum" Glossary of Chaucer,
which has so long been meditated by scholars, and for which
Prof. McElroy had been gathering material for several years.
