1895. Raleigh, Sir Walter A. The Riddle, a pleasant Pastoral Comedy
adapted from The Wife of Bath's Tale, as it is set forth in the
Works of Master Geoffrey Chaucer. Presented at Otterspool on
Midsummer’s Eve, 1895, Liverpool, 1895.
[A comedy in heroic couplets and prose, with a song. The
dedication is to Mother Eve.]
