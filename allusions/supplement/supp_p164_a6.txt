1900. Brooke, Stopford A. To F. J. Furnivall, [a poem, dated Jan. 16,
1900, in] An English Miscellany, presented to Dr. Furnivall in
honour of his 75th birthday, Oxford, 1901, p. 3.
Thou hast been friend and gossip of the dead,
Whose singing made our country like a wood.
Peopled with nightingales.   ...
Chaucer thou knewest; Shakespeare owned thy care;
We know them better for thy faithful love.
