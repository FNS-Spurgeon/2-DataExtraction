1838. Emerson, Ralph Waldo. Literary Ethics, an oration delivered
... July 24, 1838. (Works, Centenary Ed., 12 vols. ; vol. i, p. 168.)
Whilst I read the poets, I think that nothing new can be
said about morning and evening. But when I see the day¬
break I am not reminded of these Homeric, or Shakespearian,
or Miltonic, or Chaucerian pictures.
