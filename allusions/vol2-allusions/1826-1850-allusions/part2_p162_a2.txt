[p. 426] Johnson. Who would read Chaucer and Spenser for their
language?
Tooke. Spenser I would not, delightful as are many parts
of his poetry; but Chaucer I would read again and again both
for his poetry and his language.
[p. 483] Johnson. I recollect no expression in Chaucer worth
retaining and not retained.
Tooke. What think you of Swough, the long continued
sound of wind?
